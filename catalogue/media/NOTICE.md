## Which files this notice applies to

This folder now holds **two different bodies of media**, and they are under different licences:

- **4,593 exercises still carry Gym visual media.** The notice below applies to those files, in
  full and unmodified.
- **1,960 exercises were replaced** by the media cascade with material from other sources
  (RepDB, workout-guide, Everkinetic, namnq, omercotkd). Those files are **not** Gym visual's and
  are **not** covered by the notice below. Their licences and obligations are recorded in
  [`scripts/media-cascade/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../../scripts/media-cascade/ATTRIBUTION.md).

The replacement was deliberate and partial: only exercises whose name matched a cascade source were
converted, so the rest kept their original media. Until the remaining Gym visual files are licensed
or replaced, everything the notice below says continues to bind them — and it binds anyone who
copies them out of this repository.
